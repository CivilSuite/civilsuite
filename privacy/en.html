Privacy Policy — CivilSuite

Effective October 25, 2026

Who we are

CivilSuite is published by Nicolas Mathis. For any question about your data: civilsuite.app@proton.me.

The short version

CivilSuite requires no account and collects no personal data: the app sends nothing to servers we own and contains no advertising, no analytics tools and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your calculations, site reports and photos are stored and processed on your iPhone or iPad. Crack photos are analyzed on the device and are not sent to anyone.

iCloud

If you turn on iCloud backup, your data is stored in your own iCloud account through Apple's frameworks. We cannot access it. Its handling is governed by Apple's terms and privacy policy. So that a deletion applies on all your devices, the app also stores a short deletion record there (identifier and date, no content). To detect a change of iCloud account, it keeps on the device an opaque token provided by iOS, which is not sent to anyone.

Permissions we ask for

Camera: to photograph a crack or illustrate a site report. Location (only while the app is open): to get the weather and the place name for a report and, if you choose, to record where report photos were taken. To pick an existing photo, the app uses the iOS photo picker, which only passes on the photos you select: the app does not ask for access to your photo library. Each permission is optional and can be withdrawn at any time in Settings > CivilSuite.

Apple services: weather and maps

For the weather, the app sends the site location and the requested time period to Apple Weather (WeatherKit). To find the place name, its time zone or an address you type, it uses Apple Maps geocoding (MapKit). These requests are handled by Apple under its own privacy policy; we neither receive nor keep this information. Locations saved in your reports and photos stay on your device (and in your iCloud if backup is on).

Sharing PDFs and .civilsuite files

When you export a PDF or a .civilsuite file, you choose the recipient yourself (Mail, AirDrop, Files…); we have no access to it. A .civilsuite file contains the calculations and reports you selected, their photos, the author you declare (name, company), the export date and the history of previous authors. The “Remove GPS coordinates” option, on by default, removes the site and photo locations. Photos are re-encoded: only the capture date and orientation are kept. Check the content before sending: the recipient will be able to read this information.

Writing to us from the app

The feedback form prepares an email in your mail app: you review it and send it yourself. Technical information (app and iOS versions, device model, language) is attached only if you choose. We use your message and address only to reply to you.

Purchases and subscriptions

Payments are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your card or billing details. The app only checks with Apple, on your device, whether a purchase or subscription is active, so that modules can be unlocked.

Children

CivilSuite is aimed at construction and civil-engineering professionals and students. It is not designed for children and does not knowingly collect data about them.

Retention and deletion

We keep no data about you, apart from the emails you choose to send us. Deleting the app removes its local data. For iCloud data, use Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage.

Your rights

Because we do not hold your data, there is usually nothing for us to show or erase. You can still write to civilsuite.app@proton.me to exercise your rights (GDPR, LGPD, CCPA/CPRA or any applicable law) and we will reply within the legal deadlines.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with its effective date.
