Privacy Policy — CivilSuite

Effective October 23, 2026

Who we are

CivilSuite is published by [[DEV]]. For any question about your data: [[EMAIL]].

The short version

CivilSuite does not create accounts and does not collect personal data on servers we own. It contains no advertising, no analytics tools and no advertising tracking.

What stays on your device

Your calculations, assumptions, site reports and crack photos are processed on your iPhone, iPad or Apple Watch. Crack photos are analyzed on the device and are not sent to anyone.

iCloud

If you turn on backup, your data is stored in your own iCloud account through Apple's frameworks. We cannot access it. Its handling is governed by Apple's terms and privacy policy.

Permissions we ask for

Camera: to photograph a crack. Photo library: to pick an existing photo. Location (only while the app is open): to add the weather to a site report. Each one is optional and you can withdraw it at any time in Settings > CivilSuite.

Weather

To show the weather, the app sends your approximate location to a weather service provided by Apple. We neither receive nor keep this information.

Purchases and subscriptions

Payments are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your card or billing details. Through Apple, we only learn whether a purchase or subscription is active on your account, so that modules can be unlocked.

Children

CivilSuite is aimed at construction and civil-engineering professionals and students. It is not designed for children and does not knowingly collect data about them.

Retention and deletion

We keep no data about you. Deleting the app removes local data. For iCloud data, use Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage.

Your rights

Because we do not hold your data, there is usually nothing for us to show or erase. You can still write to [[EMAIL]] to exercise your rights (GDPR, LGPD, CCPA/CPRA or any applicable law) and we will reply within the legal deadlines.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with its effective date.
